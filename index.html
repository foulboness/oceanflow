Oceanographic Briefing PHYSICAL MECHANISM

The Gulf Stream is a swift, warm Atlantic western boundary current driven by wind-stress curl and intensified by Earth's rotation (Coriolis westward intensification). It transports ~30–150 Sverdrups (1 Sv = 10⁶ m³/s) of tropical heat toward Northern Europe.
